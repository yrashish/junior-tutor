Junior Tutor privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Junior Tutor is a maths practice app for children aged 5 to 11. It is made by Ashish Choudhary (aashish.choudhary1@gmail.com).

What we collect

Nothing. Junior Tutor has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers. We never receive any information about you or your child.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following on the device it runs on, and nowhere else:

  • Each child's first name, age and chosen animal, entered by a grown-up during setup.
  • Progress: which questions were answered, stars, levels and the daily goal.
  • Handwriting: the shapes of numbers your child writes, so the app can learn to read their writing.
  • Settings.

Spoken answers are turned into numbers on the device by a built-in speech model and are not saved. If the device's own speech recognition is used instead (only when the built-in model is unavailable), it is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

The app makes no network requests while your child uses it.

Deleting data

Remove a child in Settings → Children (behind the grown-ups check) to delete their data. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Because we hold no data, there is nothing to request from us.

Children's privacy

Junior Tutor is designed for children. It has no third-party advertising or analytics, and settings, and any future purchases or links, are behind a grown-ups check. Because no personal information is collected or shared, there is no information to disclose under COPPA, GDPR-K or the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. We will not start collecting data without updating this policy first.
